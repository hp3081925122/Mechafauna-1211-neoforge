Twin narrow folding bipod legs attach beneath the front jacket and fold forward under the barrel. Optional low carry handle integrated into receiver with only modest height. Conventional machine-gun proportions: long and low, about 4.2:1 length-to-total-height ratio, with barrel and jacket about half the total length and stock visibly at the opposite end. Keep these major parts clearly separated by silhouette. Absolutely no multi-barrel grouping, no gatling / minigun / rotary parts, no multiple muzzle openings, no energy gun forms, no enormous rectangular receiver, no giant decorative rings. Sparse tiny cyan sight marker if needed. Display the prop alone against a clean light neutral background. Composition: large clear three-quarter left-side view in upper 60 percent, showing muzzle pointing left, stock at right, belt and bipod clearly visible; lower strip has exact same design in orthographic left side, front muzzle view and rear view. Make views large enough to recognize the one barrel and machine-gun silhouette.
